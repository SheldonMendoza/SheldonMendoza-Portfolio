# STREAMHOMETV

**Caribbean Streaming Entertainment Platform**

- **Live site:** https://www.streamhometv.com/
- **Status:** Live, in active development
- **Profile:** https://github.com/SheldonMendoza

## Description

A Caribbean-focused streaming entertainment platform featuring original productions, television programming, music, cultural entertainment, and digital content.

StreamHomeTV includes a 24-hour live channel and radio, and streams from official YouTube sources.

## Objectives

- Provide a home for Caribbean original productions and cultural entertainment
- Bring television programming, music, and digital content together in one platform
- Build a streaming destination for Caribbean audiences and creators
