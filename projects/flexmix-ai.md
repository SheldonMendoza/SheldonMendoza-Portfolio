# FLEXMIX AI

**AI-Powered Music Production and Audio Engineering**

An AI-powered music production and audio engineering concept designed to assist with mixing, mastering, sound processing, and music production workflows.

- Support sound processing and music production workflows
- Bring AI assistance into a real Logic Pro workflow
